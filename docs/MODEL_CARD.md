# Model card: F.I.R.E.S. 7-day fire risk (v2)

Generated by `python -m godseye evaluate` from `reports/risk_evaluation.json`. Every number below comes from the **untouched test block (2026-10-02 to 2026-10-30)**, scored once.

## Intended use

A planning aid for **regional burning activity** over the next 7 days, per 0.25° cell: which areas are likely to see satellite-detected fire. It is **not** a prediction of ignitions, fire spread or danger to people, and it should not drive emergency decisions on its own.

## Data and label

- Label: any NOAA-20-VIIRS fire cell-day in the 0.25 deg cell in days t+1..t+7 (ADR 0004). One reference satellite keeps the base rate stable while satellites come and go.
- Features: NOAA-20 only, all computed from data on or before the issue date (fire days, fire cells, detections, FRP, night share, streaks, neighbouring cells, location, recent NOAA-20 outages).
- Data: NASA FIRMS VIIRS 375 m (NOAA-20, VJ114IMG), 2026-01-01 to 2026-11-06, global.
- Weekly issue dates, three blocks separated by the 7-day horizon (ADR 0005): train 2026-02-06 to 2026-08-21 (2,201,331 cell-weeks), validation 2026-08-28 to 2026-09-25 (524,795; early stopping and calibration), test 2026-10-02 to 2026-10-30 (545,802).

## Comparison with v1

v1 reported ROC-AUC 0.884 / PR-AUC 0.797 / Brier 0.114 on its own validation weeks, with early stopping on those same weeks and a different label (any sensor); not comparable and slightly optimistic.

## Limitations and failure modes

- **Persistence dominates:** recent fire in the cell and its neighbours is the strongest signal. The skill that matters is on the new-activity subset above.
- **Cells that never burned are not scored:** only cells with NOAA-20 fire since 2026-01-01 get a forecast.
- **Cropland and other managed burning count as fire:** the label is satellite-detected thermal activity, not wildfire only.
- **Clouds and outages:** a missed detection looks like no fire. NOAA-20 outages are dropped from labels, clouds are not.
- **One year of data:** no seasonal features yet. Validation and test are Aug–Oct, so other seasons are untested.

## Ethical notes

Fire is often set on purpose (agriculture, land clearing). A high score is not evidence of wrongdoing and must not be used to target communities. Public data only; no personal data.
