# Two focused areas where wildfire matters for people and forests, chosen before seeing their results

Written on Sat 14 Nov 2026, before either area's calendar, climatology or critical period was computed or looked at.

The broad named areas (Levant, Jordan, Argentina) mix very different fire regimes. Cropland burning, grassland fires and stray detections add up to "active all year" under the ADR 0006 rule. A planner needs areas small enough that their fire season means one landscape. We add two:

- **Northern Jordan forest belt:** the Irbid, Ajloun and Jerash governorates.
  - Why: they hold most of Jordan's remaining natural forest (Aleppo pine, evergreen oak). These are the hills where summer forest fires threaten villages, farms and protected woodland such as the Ajloun Forest Reserve and the Dibeen forest.
  - Box: the union of the three governorates' bounding boxes in Natural Earth 1:10m admin-1, v5.2.0-pre (ISO JO-IR, JO-AJ, JO-JA), = [35.545, 32.096, 36.094, 32.748].
  - It sits inside the Levant store and is served from it, like Jordan.
- **Paraná Delta: Islas de Victoria** (Entre Ríos, Argentina).
  - Why: the delta wetlands opposite Rosario where the 2020 fires burned for months. The smoke reached Rosario and other cities, harming air quality for over a million people.
  - Box: the bounding box of the OpenStreetMap protected area "Reserva de Uso Múltiple Islas de Victoria" (relation 8319203), = [-60.723, -33.284, -59.766, -32.432].
  - The box, unlike the polygon, also includes part of the west bank of the Paraná, including Rosario. It sits inside the Argentina store and is served from it.

The ADR 0006 rules apply unchanged. Each area's critical period is reported exactly as the rule gives it, including "active all year" or "too little detected activity".

## Considered options

- **Drawing boxes by eye around known fire hot spots:** rejected, because the boundary would be chosen with the fires in view.
- **The OSM Ramsar site "Delta del Paraná"** (relation 11179014): rejected. It covers the upper delta in Santa Fe, north of most of the 2020 burning.
- **Natural Earth geographic regions:** they name no Paraná Delta region.
